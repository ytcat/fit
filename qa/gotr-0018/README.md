# GOTR-0018 — fight straights must land with an extended arm

Base `3672c18`. Scripts in this folder; numbers are measured in the arena's
local frame on the exact frame a jab/cross registers contact, in real seeded
20-second fights run headlessly (`measure.cjs`) and in the real app in
headless Chromium (`render.cjs`). No physical phone was tested.

## Diagnosis (before, 12 seeds per case: 3, 11, 42, 74, 81, 190, 999, 2024, 7, 91, 1234, 5150)

| case | straights landed | blocked by guard | elbow at contact, blocked (p10 / median) | elbow at contact, clean (median) | clean with elbow >= 150 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Watch (AI v AI) | 133 | 97 (73%) | 95 / 114 | 133 | 4 / 35 |
| Play, lion (jab/cross presses) | 277 | 262 (95%) | 92 / 110 | 135 | 2 / 13 |
| Play, rabbit (jab/cross presses) | 297 | 262 (88%) | 94 / 112 | 142 | 11 / 33 |

- Fighters started a straight 1.22-1.27 m apart (root median; chest p10 was the
  1.02 m hard minimum). At that distance the guards touch.
- A straight's own body drive carries the shoulder 0.42 m (jab) / 0.56 m (cross)
  forward; arm + glove add 0.86 m. A fully extended straight therefore needs the
  target surface about 1.37 m from the striker's root, i.e. roots about
  1.65-1.86 m apart (body, head, raised guard). At 1.2-1.6 m the glove met the
  guard after 82% of the arm's length (elbow about 110 degrees) and was returned
  to guard from there, so the arm never straightened. This is the "90 degree"
  punch: 95% of a player's jab/cross presses ended that way.
- The earlier figure of 171 degrees was the peak one frame after contact in a
  staged clean hit, not the contact frame, and not a blocked punch.
- The integrator's hypothesis (fighters start and stay too close) is confirmed.

## Fix (all in `fight-arena.js`)

1. `keepRange()`: stance roots hold 1.74-1.88 m (opening stance included);
   each fighter gives ground in proportion to the room behind.
2. Reach controller (`straightExcess`, `updateReach`, `applyReach`): before the
   hand leaves the guard the striker's weight shifts (at most 0.26 m back,
   0.14 m forward) so the glove meets the first surface on its line in the last
   centimetres of the extension; in flight the chosen distance is held; on the
   final approach the remaining travel is compared with the free path.
3. Head straights aim at the face surface, so a raised guard still intercepts
   them at range instead of the glove passing under it.
4. A committed straight follows the opponent's steps (not a slip or roll).
5. Hooks and uppercuts step in from range (at most 0.14 m extra, inside leg reach).
6. Straights score the drive carried into the target over the last 0.1 s, so
   landing at the end of the extension does not weaken them.

  153-169 degrees and 97-100% extension.

  3 / 16 / 13 (before 3 / 6 / 4); median clean contact speed 5.9 / 5.5 / 3.9 m/s
  (before 5.7 / 5.7 / 4.0). In Watch, hooks landed clean 15 of 30 (before 9 of

## Real app (`browser.cjs`, 393x852, `ringfighterGame.quick()`)

- Play, lion, 21 jab/cross presses: 19 player straights landed, elbow median
  155 degrees, 11 at >= 150, 2 under 120, 18 with extension >= 85%; bout ended
  by health; no page errors.
- Watch: 11 straights landed, median 145 degrees, 4 at >= 150, 2 under 120;
  bout ended by health; no page errors.

## Not solved

- About one blocked straight in four still stops under 120 degrees: both
  fighters punching at once (gloves meet mid-flight) or the opponent stepping in
  with a hook/uppercut while the straight is already committed.
- A player's straights miss more often than before (about 10% against 3-4%),
  when the opponent moves off after the hand has left the guard.
- The striker's range shift happens on planted feet (a weight shift of up to
  0.26 m), not with a new step.

## Renders (real app, matched camera, contact frame)
