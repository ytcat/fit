Technique Mechanics

Kinetic Chain & Body Rotation

Push off the ball of the lead foot for the jab. On the cross, rotate the rear hip 45 degrees forward, pivot on the ball of your rear foot, and snap the right shoulder completely forward to shield your chin.

Stance & Weight Distribution

Keep 55% of your weight on the rear foot. Maintain stance width at shoulder-and-a-half. Do not lean past your lead knee on extension.

⚠️ Critical Mistakes to Avoid

Do not drop the lead hand to your chest while throwing the cross. Your left glove must stay glued to your cheekbone for guard protection.
