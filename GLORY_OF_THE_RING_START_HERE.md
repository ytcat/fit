- Audit the **current** code, branches, pull requests and CI before modifying anything. Other agents may be working in parallel. Implement incrementally, preserve existing Three.js lion/rabbit, animations, gameplay, mobile rendering, and saves.
- Prioritize functional 3-minute sessions alongside deep multi-hour progression. Keep the experience **100% free**, with no real-money purchases or paid dependencies; all in-game purchases use earned virtual currency.
- Username-based player identity, shop, fair gear, loadouts, combos, evolving rivals, original current-culture-inspired event packs, gym and academy must be playable and genuinely persistent, not static decorations.
