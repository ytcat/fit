# Bosses, scouting and story choices (approved by the owner 2026-11-12, GOTR-0062)

## Bosses: style, three phases, one tell
Level decides if you may enter; reading the boss decides if you win. Phase change = short line + camera beat + new pattern.
Three correct answers to the tell open a "weak point" window (bonus damage). Beating a boss earns his signature move.

| Boss | Style | Phase 1 | Phase 2 (60% health) | Phase 3 (25%) | Tell → answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brick (Ch.1) | Brawler | Slow heavy hooks | Charges forward | Tired, wild swings | Drops his right hand before the big hook → Duck, then Uppercut |
| King Hyena (Ch.2) | Trickster | Feints and jabs | Taunts; counters if you bite | Flurries in bursts | Laughs before a real attack → Slip, then Straight |
| Iron Bull (Ch.3) | Tank | High guard, body shots | Clinch pressure | Opens up when hit on the body | Guard rises after 3 blocks → go to the body |
| Onyx (Ch.4) | Counter-puncher | Waits for your lead | Punishes repeated moves | Speeds up | Counters a punch thrown twice → mix punches |
| Blaze (Ch.5) | Complete | Copies your most-used combo | Switches stance | Burst duels | Changes each round → use what you scouted |

Signature moves: Brick "Wrecking hook" (others to be named with their chapters).

## Scouting (optional, ~30 s, from the boss card)
Watch a short clip of the boss fighting someone (often Dash); one question with three animated answers ("What does Brick do before his big hook?").
Correct → the tell is highlighted during the fight and a notebook line is saved ("Brick: drops right hand → Duck + Uppercut"). Wrong → no hint.
The coach adds one tip based on the fighter's stats (e.g. low Stamina → "finish before phase 3").

## Story choices (one per chapter, saved in the journal, referenced later; never lock content)
| Ch. | Choice | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | After Brick: shake his hand / walk past | Handshake: Brick becomes a sparring partner (bonus training, a tip later). Walk past: he joins Dash's corner; harder rematch in chapter 3 |
| 2 | The raccoon's whistle: turn him in / let him go | Let go: street informant (free scouting). Turn in: market respect (coin bonus), Hyena crew hostile |
| 3 | Underground offer: take the dive / refuse | Dive: many coins, Pip disappointed (lose a coach bonus for a chapter). Refuse: harder Iron Bull, reputation perk |
| 4 | Press interview: humble / trash-talk Dash | Changes Dash's fighting mood and the crowd |
| 5 | Before the final: forgive Dash / finish the rivalry | Two endings |
