Gloryland

An unknown fighter arrives in Gloryland, a sunny coastal city with neon nights, and climbs from a small home gym to the Championship. This page collects every decision we made together on 11 Nov 2026. Nothing is built until you approve it.

Status: waiting for your approval Backlog: GOTR-0008 First slice: map + Home Gym + Street

What you decided

WorldIllustrated 3D city map first: tap a place to travel. Walkable streets come later, per chapter.
StoryRise to champion: an unknown fighter climbs from nothing to the title.
UnlocksMissions give stars. Enough stars lets you challenge the place's boss. Beating the boss opens the next place.
CoachesA new local coach in every place, each teaching its own style and unlocking moves of that style.
CharactersA local animal species per chapter, plus funny original characters dressed as boxers, by theme.
MissionsTraining challenges, help a character, street events, and funny tasks that are not strictly boxing.
Main settingOne big coastal city, sunny by day and neon at night. Themed zones are side missions.
GrowthMoves and combos from coaches, plus light stats from training: power, speed, stamina. Gear stays cosmetic.
LosingNo penalty. Each boss has an entry level; the higher your Fighter Level above it, the better your chance.
LengthAbout 4–6 hours for a first play-through, in pieces of 3–10 minutes.
LookDay to night by chapter: morning gym, evening street, night underground, stadium lights.
NameGloryland.

The funny tasks reuse the boxing controls: slips, footwork, timing and punches, in a different setting.

Fighter Level

Fighter Level = stars ÷ 3 (rounded down) + stat points above the start

Stars

  • Every mission gives 1–3 stars; replay to improve.
  • 18 missions in the first slice = up to 54 stars.

Stats (light)

  • Power, Speed, Stamina start at 1 and grow from training missions, up to 10 each.
  • Each point is a small edge: about +2% punch impact, +1.5% hand speed or +3% stamina.

Boss entry

  • The boss card shows its entry level, for example "Level 12".
  • Below it the button reads "Reach Level 12" and the coach suggests the best mission.

Chance to win

  • Each level above the entry makes the boss react a little slower and hit a little softer, up to 5 levels.
  • Losing costs nothing: the coach shows what went wrong and suggests one mission.

Example: 30 stars and stats Power 3, Speed 2, Stamina 2 give 10 + 4 = Level 14. That is 2 levels above King Hyena's entry.

Rules we keep

Original characters only

Funny characters evoke a genre or archetype people recognise. No names, emblems, costumes or colours of existing franchise characters, and no copy of another game's city, name, map or look. This keeps the game publishable.

Development build

While we build, every chapter and zone is open and everything is free, with price tags shown. Locks and levels apply only at go-live.

Phones first

The map is a light 3D diorama. Each place loads on its own. Walkable streets come later, only if phones keep a smooth frame rate.

Same fighting engine

Missions and bosses use the real training stations and fights, with the new game-pad controls, Burst and the coach characters.
