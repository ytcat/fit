## ACCEPTANCE TARGET (VISUAL)
The benchmark is convincing whole-body boxing: punch preparation flows from foot placement and ground force through hips, spine, shoulder, arm and wrist; the OTHER glove protects the face; head and eyes track opponents; impacts deform soft targets and immediately produce reaction in the correct direction. After a meaningful head shot the opponent may stagger, struggle to stabilize, take recovery steps, re-establish guard and resume fighting. Defense must use planted steps, slips, weaving, covers, parries and distance control; no elbow-only punches, fixed feet, frozen torso, bent-back wrists, hand inversion or teleportation. Variation should be bounded, repeatable under fixed seeds and plausible. Support lion and rabbit plus their training counterparts and any existing referee/coach as relevant.

### Phase 1 — layered pose/motion architecture
Implement reusable structured pose output, keeping present controller as legacy source/fallback:
`Pose = {rootTransform, joints:{name:quaternion}, leftFootTarget,rightFootTarget,leftHandTarget,rightHandTarget,contactFlags, expression, muscleActivation}`.
Add deterministic fixed-step simulation (recommend 1/120 or 1/60 seconds, benchmark first; capped catch-up), render-time interpolation, quaternion slerp/continuous phase transitions, seeded controlled variation, pose layers (locomotion/base, strike, defense, hit reaction, expressive secondary) and explicit priority/interrupt rules. Track grounded planted-foot anchors in world space and pelvis center-of-mass approximation; use existing two-bone IK for leg and arm constraints. Never layer two animations by writing uncoordinated Euler angles to the same joint in different modules. Use one final pose solve per frame and pass that result to skinning, shadows, muscles, collisions and targets. Define contracts and unit tests before migrating existing behavior; keep rollback flag.

### Phase 2 — authentic punches and defenses
Create data-driven action phases: anticipation/load, weight shift, launch, contact/near-miss, follow-through, recoil and recover. Different timing and joint trajectories by jab/cross/lead-hook/rear-hook/lead-uppercut/rear-uppercut; not six cosmetic variants of one arm swing. Jab: front shoulder advances, rear guard stays near cheek, lead arm extends toward actual glove target, controlled front-foot advance or planted jab, shoulder protraction and quick recoil. Cross: rear foot pivot, rear hip and thorax rotation, rear shoulder drive, lead hand high guard, aligned forearm/wrist. Hooks: elbow bent but wrist alignment maintained, rotational punch driven by hip/chest, pivot supporting foot and non-punch hand guards. Uppercut: slight knee/hip load, close-to-body curved trajectory that ends near actual target chin/torso, no exaggerated 90-degree elbow lifting and no wrist twist. Slips/weaves: hip and knee participation, head outside centerline while feet remain supported; avoid unrealistic neck bending. Guard, catch, parry, body block and retreat must visually correspond to incoming attack side and target. Feet should step, plant, pivot and recover, not slide; opposite-hand guard essential. Implement state synchronization so contact occurs at the rendered glove collision, NOT at a scripted time unrelated to geometry.

### Phase 3 — actual impact, dizzy/stagger/recovery system
Use one authoritative `CombatImpact` event: attacker, defender, punch type, hand, 3D point, surface normal, relative contact velocity, target region (head/jaw/temple/body/guard), blocked/parried/evaded flag, impact severity, direction and unique action ID. Validate contact once per action via glove swept path vs target colliders (avoid tunneling); reject false hits outside range and blocked hits as clean damage. Separate collision decision from rendered reaction. Hit response has states `stable -> flinch -> stagger -> recovering -> stable`, plus `knockdown -> count -> stand -> guard` when fight logic warrants. Clean light hit: local head/torso recoil; medium hit: neck + chest + pelvis response with guard disruption; heavy hit: weight displacement, delayed catch step, brief loss of guard, sway/dizziness, recovery, but no compulsory knockdown. Stagger direction should derive from actual impulse, balanced against footing. Use ankle/feet corrections, bounded torso lean, recovery controller and minimum interruption periods. Allow defensive interruption per rules. No instantaneous snap-back; use smooth energy decay and controlled counterbalance. Tie disorientation to existing fight stamina/stun/condition events rather than inventing unrelated health values. Different behavior for body, jaw, flank, shoulder, gloves. Add event-driven face expression (blink/squint, jaw opening, head lag), ears/mane follow-through and posture breathing. Recovery must never move a planted foot without a corresponding animated step; if balance cannot be restored, trigger proper knockdown transition. Tests must include repeated hits, clashes, edge-of-ring movement, interruptions and no infinite dizzy loop.

### Phase 4 — geometry, deformation and realistic material response
Inspect lion/rabbit original models from all angles and clay/shadow views. Prioritize silhouette and articulation: realistic neck/shoulder transitions, trapezius and chest, shoulder girdle and armpit folds, hip/knee anatomy, properly fitted gloves/boots, facial proportions and non-faceted ears/muzzle. Repair any holes, nonmanifold edges, penetration, detached accessories, oddly geometric referee or opponent. Preserve or enhance GPU skinning and current local corrections; avoid disconnected sphere/cylinder muscles. Muscle visual response should be pose-driven per group (deltoid, pec, triceps/biceps, oblique, abs, back, thigh, calf) with anatomically plausible modest deformation; never inflate whole limbs. Use neutral expression and combat expression blends. Improve micro-roughness/normal textures and materials (fur, mane, cloth, leather, rubber); specular properties must match materials. Use coherent contact shadows, effective key/fill/rim light and appropriately filtered shadows; maintain correct linear/sRGB color workflow. Manage levels of detail by projected size and mobile GPU. Preserve existing glove/bag deformation, ensure collision points and depth/shadow geometry use same coordinate-space deformation. The target is improved BELIEVABILITY, not arbitrary polygon count.
