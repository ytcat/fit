GOTR-0031 · build v1.19 · proposal for review

One next step, every round

I played Glory of the Ring as a brand-new player on a 402×690 phone screen and on desktop, recording every screen and decision. Below: how the game works today, where a new player gets lost, and a simpler path where the player always knows the one thing to do next.

Today
After naming, you land on an idle lion with four equal buttons. The coach punches and fights for you unless you find Play. After every activity a busy hub pops up and hides who won.
VS
Proposed
A Next up card gives one clear step. You throw the punches from the first second. Every round ends on its own result card, followed by a reward and the next step.

Round 1 · Today

The game as it is

Three tabs (Train, Fight, World), a header with Shop and the player's name, and a Training drawer behind the station button. Most features live in one World hub dialog that also opens by itself after every activity.

Where players get lost

Friction, with evidence

Tap a screenshot to enlarge it. All were taken from the real app at 402×690.

F1High

No first step after naming

You land on an idle lion with four buttons of equal weight: station, Train 15s, Play and Fight Academy. A guided intro, "Your first rounds", does exist, but it sits in World › School behind Start guide.

Fix: #3 first-run path, #4 Next up card

F2High

"Train 15s" trains without you

Unless Play is on, the coach shadowboxes alone and the button counts down. Turning Play on changes its label to "Watch", so the label always names the mode you are not in.

Fix: #2 you control by default

F3High

Your first fight is fought by the coach

Fights start under coach control. Pressing Play opens the Combos page, which is empty for a new player (only "Choose combos"), so there are no punch buttons until you find Moves.

Fix: #2, #10 empty states

F4High

The result is hidden

After every session or bout the World hub pops up. The reward card comes second, under a coaching tip, and for a fight it says "Activity complete · +10 XP · 0/0 · 20s" without saying who won. The real result ("Shadow won · health decision") sits underneath, with the disabled punch buttons on top of it.

Fix: #1 result card

F5Medium

One place, four names

School, Club, Boxing Club and Your corner all mean the player's place. Go to Club opens the Shop. Record book opens "Activity history". Fight controls ▾ opens a drawer titled "Training" that starts at "03 / Your session".

Fix: #5 one name per thing, #7 navigation

F6Medium

Three ways to get one combo

Each combo card offers a requirement ("Complete two rope drills 0/2"), Buy and Learn in Academy. "First Rhythm" is also the name of a lesson and of a training tick.

Fix: #9 clear combo route

F7Medium

Language is three levels deep

Station ▾ › View & sound › Settings opens a dialog called "Practice controls" with EN/HE/RU, a second sound toggle, a "Lion" button, "Resume motion" and an unexplained "28-day plan · 0/28".

Fix: #6 settings sheet

F8Medium

Developer notes on player screens

"No adaptive learning yet." · "simulated practice: 0" · "No bonus currency or mastery is awarded." · "While you were away" on day one · "Cross-device accounts require the account service to be connected."

Fix: #5 copy pass

F9Medium

The fight choice is only about length

20 s, 40 s, 60 s or 2 × 20 s, where "40 seconds" and "2 × 20 seconds" read the same. You choose the opponent (Rivals, Cup) and the arena somewhere else, in the World hub.

Fix: #11 Next bout screen

F10Medium

Nothing to aim for

All nine arenas, the Championship Arena included, are free from the first minute. The arc from neighbourhood hall to the big stage has no steps.

Fix: #8 progressive unlocks

F12Low

Hebrew gaps

The main button still says "Train 15s" in Hebrew, and the school name stays "Maya School". After the hub closes, the World tab stays highlighted over the Train scene.

Fix: #5

F14Low

Short on coins, with no way forward

"Need 25 more ◈" does not say how to earn it. The Shop header also holds Player & backup, Record book and an unlabeled ⤓ for the offline pack.

Fix: #10 empty states, #6

Also logged in docs/ux/UX_FLOWS.md: F11 (fighter choice hidden; rival cards switch your species), F13 (Export/Import backup on first launch; × makes a silent Guest), F15 (cryptic "0/3 · 15s" and "health decision"). Duplicate paths: the Academy can be reached from 3 places, the Shop from 2 and training from 3.

Round 2 · Proposed

Five tabs, one next step

The 3D gym and the fighter stay on the home screen. The World hub is split up so that each feature lives where a player would look for it, and settings move into one sheet.

The first five minutes

Make the first purchase reachable

Today a session pays 7 ◈, a lesson 15 ◈ and a lost fight 0 ◈, while the cheapest gloves cost 25 ◈. Either pay 3–5 ◈ for finishing any bout or price one starter item at about 20 ◈. The exact numbers need balancing.

Reuse what exists

The "Your first rounds" tutorial state, Academy lesson 1 (watch → repeat ×3), the 15-second session, the 20-second bout and the Shop confirm step already work. The new part is the order, and taking the player straight from one step to the next.

The core loop

How Next up decides

Suggest the Academy when

  • a lesson would unlock a combo you don't have and you haven't done a lesson today
  • you lost on defence (for example, took twice as many hits as you landed)
  • two fights in a row passed without using a combo

Suggest a fight when

  • a combo was just earned ("try it")
  • two or more training sessions since the last fight
  • a rival or the next cup bout is ready

Corner talk

Three questions for you

  1. Can the 9 arenas become earned rewards for new players? Existing players would keep all of them.
  2. Should the first screen ask the player to choose Lion or Rabbit, and should rival cards stop switching the player's species?
  3. Which single name for the player's place: Gym (proposed), Club (matches "Boxing Club") or Corner?

How this was made. Build v1.19 (commit b8426a2) was run locally and driven with Playwright as a new player at 402×690 (touch) and 1366×768, in English with a Hebrew spot-check. Software 3D rendering in the test browser is slow, so I moved the training clock forward and stepped the fight ahead; screens and rewards were not changed. The third Academy repetition did not register at the low frame rate, so the "Lesson passed" screen is described from the code. Nothing was tested on a physical phone.
