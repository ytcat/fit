# R11: extended exercise ranges and smoother steps

Uppercuts strike with the fist, driven by knees, hips and trunk, with a bent arm.
The animation now reaches higher and farther forward during the upward drive;
the elbow remains bent at contact. It does not turn the uppercut into an elbow strike.

- Jab/cross: greater forward pelvis transfer and shoulder protraction with fixed
  limb lengths, retained guard and nearly full elbow extension.
- Lead/rear hook: increase shoulder-to-wrist radius to 0.615 m; retain a bent
  elbow, curved fist travel, driving-foot pivot and body turn.
- Lead/rear uppercut: raise the wrist target from 1.78 to 1.84 m, advance it from
  0.46 to 0.60 m, increase hip/trunk turn and driving-foot pivot. Keep the loading
  dip and direct guard recovery.
- Slip/roll: modestly enlarge lateral travel and knee lowering, retaining gaze
  and cheek-following guard.
- Footwork: increase each step from 0.12 to 0.16 m. Use squared half-sine lift
  so take-off and landing have zero vertical velocity; retain ordered feet.

These are model-specific authored choices rather than prescribed exercise ranges.
The original quintic interpolation and geometric wrist/foot constraints remain.
Tests cover all nine techniques, combos, long-hook elbow bend, fist orientation,
uppercut stroke and bent-arm contact, stride, smooth take-off and guard recovery.
Uppercut and footwork software pose renders were inspected; this does not validate
live WebGL appearance or frame rate.
