# Crowd people checkpoint (branch `agent/crowd-people`)

Owner, 2026-11-21: "The people in the background, the crowd is very bad. It's just geometric figures... Please build them much better."

- **Changed:** `arena-sets.js` only (plus `index.html` `arena-sets.js?v=2026112181` and the regenerated `offline-manifest.json`). `Kit.person()` now queues a spectator; `Kit.flushPeople()` (called from `finish()`) builds them from shared prototypes: anthropomorphic animal heads (lion, rabbit, bear, wolf, fox, otter, deer, dog: skull, muzzle, nose, eyes with glint, species ears, lion mane), bodies per pose and outfit (torso shaped at hips/waist/chest/shoulders, neck, two bent arms with hands, two bent legs with shoes; tee, hoodie with hood, open jacket over a shirt, hi-vis vest with stripes, overalls with straps), caps, beanies, hard hats, a folding chair for seated guests. 14 standing poses (arms crossed, hands in pockets, hands on hips, one fist / both arms raised, pumping fist, clapping, pointing, filming with a phone held low or high) and 9 seated poses. The phone glow of the ringless crowds sits on the phone in the hand. Prototype vertices carry a palette slot, `Kit.add` colours them from the person's palette; everything still merges into the per-sector `a`/`b`/`s` batches (sway and culling unchanged, no new draw calls or materials).
- **Levels of detail:** three; the set's triangle budget (`peopleBudget` 57,000 per built venue, under the 60,000 phone cap in `tests/arena-sets.test.cjs`) is filled closest-first: the front row of the close circles at full detail, the rest at the middle or far level. Championship seats are now 0.62 m apart (was 0.56) so its 204 guests fit.
- **Checks:** `node tests/arena-crowd.test.cjs` (new), `node tests/arena-sets.test.cjs`, `bash scripts/run-tests.sh -j 2 arena venue ringless docks fight`.
