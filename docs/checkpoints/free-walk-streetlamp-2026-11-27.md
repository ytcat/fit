# Free Walk street lamps: candidate, not shipped
Owner described a yellow bubble, lighting on the avatar, and apparent leakage past walls. The old Free Walk literal warm point light was attached to the player each frame; regular lamp bulb sprites were large additive billboards. Remove those from Walk only. Retain cool moon fill, actual lamp geometry and overview glows; project a restrained, soft ground pool fixed under each regular map lamp, with conservative clipping against the existing solid height field. One batch and no extra shadow pass. Special venue and neon lighting are intentionally unchanged. The nearest render surface may still expose small missed/incomplete patches; visual approval is open.

Tests: street-lamp-pools (synthetic up-to-and-beyond-wall geometry, depth state, water rejection); walk-night-lights (overview stays 1,180, 304 parked-car points excluded from Walk, 60 map-lamp halos replaced by ground lighting); walk-crossings (1,259 stripes per view, no overlaps). Shader and world appearance were not run on a real renderer. Next: compare same matched nighttime player walk on both sides of a wall near a regular lamp, verify visibility, fades and both entry orders on mobile. No published version bump; integrate only after real gameplay QA and approval.
